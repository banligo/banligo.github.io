**Extracting the Imprint of Gravitational Waves from Galaxy Alignments in the Large-Scale Structure** Teppei Okumura (ASIAA)

This talk explores novel methods to detect gravitational wave signatures through galaxy alignment correlations in large-scale structure observations.

Intrinsic Alignment (IA) Effects:
- Intrinsic ellipticity auto-correlation (II) of elliptical galaxies and their host halos observed in SDSS data
- Filament effect: galaxies tend to align with cosmic web filaments
- Linear Alignment (LA) model describes projected galaxy/halo shapes (Catelan, Kamionkowski framework)

Theoretical Framework:
- Okumura, Matsubara, Eisenstein (2009): Analysis of anisotropy in galaxy-intrinsic (GI) correlations in 3D space
- Projected tidal force field modeling in three dimensions
- Overlap Reduction Functions (ORFs) derived from:
  - $\langle h^T h^T \rangle$ correlations (standard GW signatures)
  - $\langle h^T h \rangle$ correlations (parity-violating GW effects)

Novel Detection Methods:
- Divergence-Curl Decomposition: Taking $\nabla \cdot \nabla \times$ of the projected tidal tensor field enables local extraction of GW signals while filtering out scalar gravitational potential contributions
- Cross-Correlation Technique: Computing $\langle \nabla f \cdot \nabla \times x \rangle$ allows isolation of parity-violating GW signatures
- These methods provide optimal estimation strategies for GW effects on intrinsic alignments

Significance:
This approach offers a complementary pathway to traditional GW detection, utilizing the cosmic web's response to gravitational waves through galaxy orientation patterns.
